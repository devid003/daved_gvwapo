What the colors mean

Green — online, everything working.
Yellow — slow (high ping); prompts and the website may lag.
Red — down; prompts can't be used and the website is unavailable.

The bot refreshes the Discord message on the interval you set per server below. A fully-down bot can't post, so if the time on the message gets old, it's down — this page always shows the real current color. On a planned restart or deploy the bot posts Red before it goes offline.
