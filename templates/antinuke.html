Anti-nuke

Stops someone from wrecking your server in seconds. If one person suddenly deletes a bunch of channels or roles, or mass-bans members, the bot catches it, stops them, and alerts you.

{{ flash }}

Before you rely on this, set up the bot's role:
  1. In Server Settings → Roles, drag the bot's role near the top, above your admins and mods.
  2. Give that role View Audit Log (to see who did it) plus Ban Members, Kick Members and Manage Roles (to stop them).
  3. Whitelist the people you trust below (you and your real admins), so the bot never touches them. The owner is always safe.
It can't act on the server owner or on roles higher than its own, and it can't bring back deleted channels or roles — it stops the attacker fast, it doesn't undo the damage.
